Optimal Waveform Design & Inverse Problems in Dispersive Media

J. H. Morales Bárcenas · Departamento de Matemáticas, UAM Iztapalapa

Variational formulation of waveform design in tomographic imaging through dispersive media, minimizing the mean-square reconstruction error $\mathbb{E}\bigl[\|\hat{f} - f\|_2^2\bigr]$ under single-scattering and colored thermal noise models.
